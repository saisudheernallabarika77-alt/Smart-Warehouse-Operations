# WarehouseIQ — Flow Command Presentation

## Slide 1: Intelligent fulfillment architecture replaces passive warehouse tracking with proactive operational decisions
- Traditional warehouse management systems act as passive record-keeping databases, leaving fulfillment leads to resolve priority clashes manually.
- WarehouseIQ shifts fulfillment operations from reactive firefighting to real-time, decision-driven flow command.
- The platform unifies inventory visibility, automated order prioritization, and pick-wave optimization into a single control desk.

## Slide 2: Fragmented inventory visibility and priority clashes cause costly SLA breaches and missed carrier cut-offs
- Disconnected stock counts between available, reserved, and inbound deliveries lead to hidden stockouts during peak hours.
- Urgent orders frequently clash with standard queues, forcing fulfillment leads to pause operations while searching for inventory.
- Unmanaged quality-check queues and unoptimized picking routes accumulate minutes of avoidable dwell time per shift.

## Slide 3: WarehouseIQ establishes a unified control room that protects shift promises and optimizes floor execution
- The central command desk provides instant visibility into active orders, pick accuracy, floor capacity, and bottleneck recovery time.
- Persistent operations rails anchor shift tracking, active facility location, and lead profile management across every module.
- Live telemetry connects executive metrics directly to floor-level task execution without unnecessary tab switching.

## Slide 4: Multi-tier prioritization and promise-date ranking safeguard urgent customer commitments
- Orders are automatically categorized into Urgent, High, and Standard tiers based on promise dates and carrier cut-offs.
- Live SLA risk indicators highlight potential delivery failures before they impact customers.
- Interactive order-detail drawers provide complete visibility into item allocations, picking zones, and fulfillment-chain steps.

## Slide 5: Proactive split-allocation logic transforms inventory exceptions into guided operator resolutions
- When urgent demand exceeds available shelf stock, the system evaluates inbound replenishment and lower-priority reservations.
- Exception workflows guide fulfillment leads through split-allocation recommendations rather than failing silently.
- Approving an allocation resolution instantly logs an audit trail and updates active pick waves without breaking floor rhythm.

## Slide 6: Zone-based picking sequences eliminate duplicate aisle travel and accelerate picker productivity
- Floor execution routes sequence items by zone and carrier cut-off to keep warehouse walkers highly productive.
- Recommended pick waves bundle urgent allocations with nearby orders, removing 126 meters of duplicate travel per run.
- Live task boards display picker assignments, zone statuses, and station completion rates in real time.

## Slide 7: Integrated dispatch tracking aligns dock bay capacity and quality checks with strict carrier cut-offs
- Outbound control boards join express, priority, and ground manifests with dock assignments and loading statuses.
- Quality-check load balancing prevents bottlenecks at inspection stations before express carrier vans depart.
- Carrier confidence scoring forecasts on-time shipping performance based on active shift throughput.

## Slide 8: Actionable operational intelligence isolates recoverable minutes and measures true throughput
- Flow analytics focus on throughput versus operating targets rather than vanity metrics.
- Bottleneck watch panels highlight exactly where time accumulates across quality checks, travel distance, and allocation holds.
- Historical shift comparisons evaluate intervention effectiveness and guide continuous process improvement.

## Slide 9: A robust modern tech stack delivers high-performance reliability with an editorial signal-room aesthetic
- Built on React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 4, and shadcn/ui component primitives for lightning-fast responsiveness.
- The Signal Room design system features warm paper surfaces, deep ink command rails, and high-contrast signal amber alerts.
- Fully version-controlled and deployed with continuous integration ready for enterprise fulfillment scaling.
